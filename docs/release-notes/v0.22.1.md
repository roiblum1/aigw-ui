# 0.22.1 (not released yet)

Fixes to the budget across models of 0.22.0, found in an end-to-end test on
the lab gateway on 2026-10-16. In 0.22.0 the budget could not be set on the
sandbox hub, would not have been charged on any entry route, and moved
tenants onto each other's counters.

Database: migration 026 gives each tenant's budget a fixed position in the
gateways' policy, `tenants.budget_slot`. Today's budgets keep the positions
they have.

## Upgrading

The counters of budgets across models get new names (the tenant is now part
of them), so what tenants used of their budgets in the running period is
counted from 0 once. Quotas per model are not affected.

A route of a cluster's own that has a `BackendTrafficPolicy` of its own needs
`mergeType: StrategicMerge` on it; the sync now reports the budget policy
`Overridden` when one does not. See
[deployment](../deployment.md#before-the-first-budget-across-models), which
also says where the shared Redis should be and what timeout to give it.

## Fixed

- **The budget was not charged on entry routes.** The policy of an entry
  route or a best-effort route replaced the budget policy on that route:
  Envoy Gateway reported the budget policy `Overridden`, and no request
  through an entry route was charged or refused. Those policies now have
  `mergeType: StrategicMerge`. The sync reported the budget policy as
  accepted; it now reports `Overridden` as a rejection with Envoy Gateway's
  message, which names the routes, and the self-test says so.
- **A budget or a list of models could not be set** while any model had a
  standard or any tenant already had a budget: the endpoints answered 409.
  They used the check for a new API key. They now check that the gateways
  take tokens only, and nothing else.
- **Tenants moved onto each other's counters.** The rules were in slug
  order and a counter is named after its rule's position, so a tenant added,
  removed or disabled before others moved every later one onto its
  neighbour's counter: on the lab a tenant's first request ever was refused
  with another tenant's spend. Each budget now keeps a position, a free
  position is a rule that matches no tenant, and the rule also matches the
  tenant header as `Distinct`, which puts the tenant into the counter's
  name. A new amount keeps what the tenant used.
- **A route of the cluster's own serving several priced models** was given
  the prices of whichever model the sync wrote last. It now gets one
  expression that charges each model its own prices.
- **Prices in a dry run charged the budgets.** A dry run refuses nobody for
  the model's prices; its routes now write no cost until it ends.
- The tenant's page said that changing a budget starts its count over, and
  both pages that models without prices are outside the budget. A new
  amount keeps the count; models without prices cost nothing from it but
  are refused once it is spent, like every other model.

## Changed

- Every answer of the server carries `X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff`,
  `X-Frame-Options: SAMEORIGIN` with `Content-Security-Policy:
  frame-ancestors 'self'`, and `Referrer-Policy: no-referrer`. Before, only
  the platform page had a policy: another site could show the admin pages in
  a frame.

## Tested

On the lab gateway (Envoy Gateway 1.9.2, AI Gateway 1.2.0 with the lab
patches), with the policies this version renders applied by hand to a test
listener, stand-in models priced at $15/$1.50/$75 (opus), $3/$0.30/$15
(sonnet) and $1/$0.10/$5 (haiku) per million tokens, and the hub's real
cost expressions on the routes:

- A tenant held to opus: opus 200; sonnet, haiku, a model without prices
  and an unknown model 403; the same on `/anthropic/v1/messages`. A tenant
  held to opus and sonnet: both 200, streaming too; haiku 403. `GET
  /v1/models` is answered for both.
- Every charge exact to the credit on chat, streaming chat, Anthropic and
  streaming Anthropic. 10,000 input tokens of which 8,000 cached cost 4,200
  credits against 15,000 uncached: the cached part at a tenth.
- A $1 budget across models: opus and sonnet charged together, the request
  after it was spent refused with 429, then haiku, a model without prices
  and the Anthropic path refused too; other tenants not affected. A $0.20
  quota on opus refused opus while the budget still had $0.23, and sonnet
  spent the rest.
- A route with its own policy: not charged without `mergeType` (status
  `Overridden`), charged and refused with it, its own retry policy kept; no
  change where the listener has no policy.
- A tenant's position: its first request refused with another tenant's
  spend under 0.22.0's rendering, served from a fresh counter with the
  tenant's `Distinct` match.

Not tested on a gateway: migration 026 on a hub with budgets, and the new
self-test steps, which run with the hub after this is deployed. The Usage
page's reading of the new counter names follows Envoy Gateway's source; the
self-test's budget step checks it against Redis.
