# 0.16.0 (not released yet)

Every LLMInferenceService measured on its own, with one versioned benchmark
profile, drained or live. Step 2 of the
[design](../design/capacity-prices-wallets.md).

Database: migration 020 adds `benchmark_profiles` (with version 1, the 0.13
defaults with steps of at most five minutes), the spec hash, quantization,
LMCache and decode replicas of each deployment, and a run's spec hash,
profile, divisor, phase and who started it.

## Upgrading

Measurements by shape, from 0.13–0.14, stop counting the moment the hub
runs 0.16, all at once:

- **Site weights** of every model whose weights came from measurements go
  back to the annotation `aigw-ui.io/capacity-per-instance`, or to 1 an
  instance without it, at once and not one instance a poll, until every
  ready LLMI of the model is measured again.
- **What a model can be given a day** falls to $0, and the budget planner
  in 0.17 with it, until its LLMIs are measured.
- Nothing a tenant pays or may spend changes: prices and budgets are not
  touched.

Before upgrading a hub whose site weights come from measurements, set
`aigw-ui.io/capacity-per-instance` on its LLMIs to the ratio you want in
the meantime, and plan to measure each LLMI soon after: by hand, or by
setting `capacity.window`.

## What changed

- **Keyed by LLMInferenceService.** A result belongs to one LLMI (cluster,
  namespace, name) and what it runs: SHA-256 of its `spec` without any
  `replicas`, of the `LLMInferenceServiceConfig`s its `baseRefs` name, and
  of the image digests its ready model servers run, plus the
  prefill:decode ratio. Scaling keeps a result; changing an argument, a
  shared config or the image makes the LLMI not measured. During a rollout
  the hash it had is kept, and the LLMI is not measured until its pods
  agree. Two LLMIs of one
  model on two sites are measured and shown apart. The page shows each
  LLMI's quantization and whether it uses LMCache. Measurements by shape from
  0.13–0.14 stay in **Measurements** as history and count for nothing.
- **One benchmark profile, versioned.** **Benchmark profile** on the
  Capacity page edits the workload, the targets and the sweep; saving makes
  a new version. A model is counted with one version only: after a change
  the old results keep counting, marked stale, until the new version covers
  more of its ready instances.
- **The sweep** doubles the requests in flight until a step misses a target,
  then halves the gap twice. The result is the highest step that met the
  targets below the lowest that missed.
- **Drained or live.** Drained, the default, takes the site's entry weight to
  0 with the existing drain, starts the Job once it is 0, waits for the pod
  to have no request in flight, and gives the weight back. It needs another
  site serving the model, and is refused otherwise. The drain is made and
  ended in the transactions that record and end the measurement, so a hub
  that stops never leaves a site drained. What a model can be given still
  counts the site while a measurement drains it. Live measures while the LLMI
  serves users, after a warning and the model's name typed, and is marked
  live; its throughput is the pod's own counters over the time AIPerf had
  requests out.
- **Other traffic** is the larger of two estimates: what the pod counted in
  the step beyond what was sent, allowing the longest prompt for each of the
  measurement's own failed requests; and, drained, what it counted in a
  20-second quiet spell before the step, carried over the step. Drained, a
  step with more than 5% from elsewhere fails as contaminated, after the
  check of failed requests; live, the share is shown.
- **Prefill/decode** is measured through its scheduler on the gateway's peer
  listener, from the gateway's namespace, with the peer client certificate
  mounted read-only, and divided by its decode replicas. The Job's proxy
  runs in 8 processes, keeps and pre-opens its connections, and the Job asks
  for 4 CPUs.
- **Stale results.** A new profile version or a result older than 30 days
  marks it stale; a changed spec drops it.
- **Measuring by itself.** With `capacity.window` set in the chart, such as
  `"Fri 22:00 - Sat 06:00 Asia/Jerusalem"`, the hub measures, drained, what
  is not measured and then what is stale, one per site and per model at a
  time. A model served on one site waits for an operator, and so does one
  whose other sites, by their measurements, cannot take its busiest hour in
  the window over the last 30 days. The page shows the window, the queue and
  that share. Empty, the default, never.
- **Site weights** from measurements now take each LLMI's result, still only
  when every ready LLMI of the model has one.
- API: `GET /api/v1/benchmark-profiles`, `PUT /api/v1/benchmark-profile`;
  `POST /models/{id}/capacity-runs` takes `mode` and `confirm` instead of
  `settings`; `GET /capacity` returns `llmis`, `profile` and `campaign`.

## Tested

- **Units:**
  - the spec hash on the lab's real LLMInferenceService: scaling keeps it; one more argument, metadata, the prefill:decode ratio; a change in a base config or the image; a base config from the LLMI's namespace before KServe's; the images by role; none while a rollout runs, none ready, or listing failed;
  - quantization and LMCache read from args and `VLLM_ADDITIONAL_ARGS`;
  - the profile's validation and scaling to a short model;
  - the sweep (1, 2, 4 missing, then 3) and the analysis of bisected steps;
  - the contamination check: 7% fails drained, 2.4% passes, live is shown; two failed requests within their allowance are not contamination; ten are reported as failed first; a quiet spell at 1,000 tokens a second passes (2.4%), at 5,000 fails (12%);
  - live throughput from the pod's counters, and the division over decode replicas;
  - the window, weekly and daily, across midnight and the end of the week;
  - which result counts: two sites apart, a changed spec, a profile change with the old version kept until the new one covers more, results older than 30 days, prefill/decode;
  - the campaign queue: missing before stale, waits for a model, a site, an operator; covered or not, in tokens and in credits; a drained or unmeasured other site takes nothing; traffic outside the window or older than 30 days does not count;
  - a site drained by a measurement still counts in what the model can be given; one an operator drained does not;
  - the drained flow: drain, Job only at weight 0, restore; a drain an operator made stays; the last site is refused before anything is recorded; a 30-minute timeout; cancel while draining; an LLMI whose spec hash is not known is refused;
  - a prefill/decode Job in the gateway namespace with the peer certificate;
  - the live confirmation (the name, case and all).
- **Runner, with a stand-in for vLLM and a fake AIPerf:**
  - the sweep, and the length check;
  - a stand-in that other clients reach: drained, the quiet spell sees it and the step fails as contaminated; live passes and shows it;
  - an instance that never runs dry is not measured; one that other clients start to reach during the run fails the next step;
  - the counters read through the step and taken over AIPerf's window (checked against the real AIPerf in CI: the counters' time equals AIPerf's `benchmark_duration`).
- **Prefill/decode proxy:**
  - a TLS stand-in that demands a client certificate signed by its CA got the requests of three steps through the runner's proxy, with the peer SNI and Host, and four connections opened before the step at 4;
  - measured on Linux (podman, Python 3.12) against a Go stand-in streaming a token every 20 ms, at 64 to 512 streams. Before: first-token p95 5.4 s at 64 streams and 27.6 s at 256 (49 and 85 ms direct), from a listen backlog of 5, a TLS handshake per request and Nagle's algorithm; between tokens p95 56 ms at 512 even on 8 CPUs, one process. After, on 4 CPUs: between tokens p95 21.4 ms (21.5 direct) and first token p95 22–38 ms at every level.
- **Store, on Postgres:**
  - migration 020 and the seeded profile;
  - a new profile version made active, the earlier ones kept;
  - runs keyed by LLMI, with shape runs from before left out;
  - phases, the spec hash on deployments.
- **On the lab**, a hub on this branch with sync off. It reads the lab and creates only the Job.
  - Discovery read `vllm-lab/qwen-tiny` with spec `2fde1bc93dc9…`.
  - A drained measurement was refused: no other site serves it. The campaign queue said it waits for an operator.
  - A live one was refused without the model's name, and with it in another case.
  - With the name, it ran on a small profile version 2 for the CPU lab (prompts up to 2,000 tokens, steps of 2 minutes, at most 8 in flight). It swept 1, 2, 4 and 8, all within that profile's targets: p95 4.5–8.6 s to the first token, about 2 s between tokens, 3–25 requests a step, 0% other traffic.
  - The page shows it measured, live, with profile version 2, and counts 8 in flight at 4.3M tokens a day for the instance.
  - The Measure form offered live only, with the warning and the name to type.

## Not tested

- **A drained run on a real gateway**: the lab is one site, so its model cannot be drained; the drained flow, the quiet spell and the cover check are tested with fakes and stand-ins.
- **A crash between the two writes of a drain**: the transaction is tested on Postgres; the hub was not killed mid-run.
- **Base configs on a real cluster**: the lab's LLMI names none.
- **A prefill/decode LLMI on a real cluster**: the lab has none. The peer path is tested against a TLS stand-in; that vLLM's prompt counter on decode pods counts what the measurement sent is assumed. The proxy was measured against a stand-in, not a real model.
- **Changing an argument of the lab's LLMI**: not done, to leave the lab model running; it is tested on its real spec.
- **The real AIPerf, on this Mac**: it crashes in qemu (amd64 emulated on arm64) while exporting a step, before any result. CI runs it natively.
- **The campaign window on a real cluster.**
