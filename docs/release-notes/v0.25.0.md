# 0.25.0 (2026-10-16)

A tenant is held on the models with prices one way or the other: one budget
across models, any amount, for every model with prices together, or a
budget of its own per model, each with its own amount. Never both. With a
budget across models the tenant chooses what to spend it on: the models'
standards and pools no longer hold it.

Database: nothing new.

## Upgrading

- Nothing to do. A tenant that has both a budget across models and quotas
  of its own on models with prices keeps both stored; its page marks the
  quotas, which hold nothing from now on, and its budget cannot be changed
  until they are removed.
- While any tenant has a budget across models, the first sync after the
  upgrade adds one rule at the end of each priced model's `QuotaPolicy`.
  Its pool's count starts again, as it does whenever the number of rules
  changes; the tenants' own counts do not. A model with prices and no
  quota or standard gets a `QuotaPolicy` of its own, so that its pool (1 by
  default) holds the tenants with neither a budget nor a quota there: give
  those tenants a budget, a quota or a standard first.

## Changed

- **A budget across models or a budget per model, not both.** The hub
  refuses a budget across models while the tenant has quotas of its own on
  models with prices (also prices that wait for their day), and such a
  quota while the tenant has a budget (HTTP 409); the message says what to
  remove. Quotas on models without prices stay possible beside a budget:
  the budget does not count those models. Before, both held and the
  tighter won, so a quota above the budget held nothing.
- **A budget across models is the tenant's only limit there.** Each priced
  model's `QuotaPolicy` ends with a rule that matches a token with a budget
  (`x-aigw-budget`) per tenant and never runs out; in `Shared` mode that
  lets the request past the model's standard, its pool and any quota left
  from before. Before, a tenant with $5 an hour across models could spend
  no more than Opus's $2 standard on Opus once Opus's pool was used.
- The tenant's page shows **Budget across models** above **Quotas**. With a
  budget, the quota form offers models without prices only and says why;
  with quotas on priced models, the budget card names them instead of
  **Set a budget**. A quota left from before is tagged **holds nothing:
  budget across models**, with Remove only.
- The Usage page shows a tenant's use of a model inside its budget across
  models as "in its budget across models", without the model's limit; the
  tenant's own page (`/my/usage`, the Claude Code and omp plugins) leaves
  those models out of `budgets` and shows the budget across models.
- The self-test's budget step takes its tenant's quota away instead of
  raising it: the first request is answered past the model's own rules,
  the budget alone refuses the next.
- The Models page counts the new rule.
