# 0.15.0 (2026-10-15)

Market prices. A model's default price is now its price on OpenRouter, used
as it is, so a dollar of a budget means what the same traffic would cost
there and the models compare as the market does. Step 3 of the
[design](../design/capacity-prices-wallets.md).

Database: migration 019 adds `models.market_id`,
`model_deployments.market_id` and `model_prices.market`.

## What changed

- **A snapshot of the market is built in.** `internal/market/openrouter.json`
  holds OpenRouter's public catalog of 2026-10-15 (455 models with a
  price), embedded in the server: a hub with no network has it, and the
  build fetches nothing. `hack/fetch-market.sh` writes it on a machine with
  internet access. A variable price (`-1`) counts as none; long-context
  tiers are ignored.
- **Each model finds its entry**: one chosen in the Prices dialog, else the
  annotation `aigw-ui.io/market-id` on its LLMInferenceServices, else a name
  it is served under that is an entry's Hugging Face id. Of entries that
  share a Hugging Face id, such as `z-ai/glm-5.3` and `z-ai/glm-5.3:batch`,
  the one without a `:` variant is taken.
- **The Prices dialog** has a Market line with the entry, how it was found,
  the snapshot's date and the market's prices, and **Use market prices**,
  which fills them in. Saving follows the usual rules: the next 00:00 UTC,
  the first prices convert the limits once and start in dry-run.
  **Choose the market entry** searches the snapshot, or sets none, or goes
  back to automatic.
- **Nothing changes a price by itself.** Prices saved unchanged from the
  market remember it. When a newer snapshot has other prices, the Models
  page shows **market moved** and the dialog offers the new ones.
- **A new snapshot every week**: the workflow `market` opens or updates the
  pull request "Market snapshot YYYY-MM-DD". A version tag fails CI with a
  snapshot older than 60 days (`hack/check-market.sh`), and
  `deploy/offline/build-bundle.sh` reads a fresh one before building.
- API: `GET /api/v1/market?q=`, `PUT /api/v1/models/{id}/market-id`, and
  on every model `market`, `market_note`, `market_id_set` and
  `market_annotations`; a set of prices carries `market`.

## Tested

- `internal/market`:
  - the embedded snapshot parses, and has no negative price;
  - matching by Hugging Face id, the `:batch` variant, two base entries with one id, the 60-day age;
  - the cached price taken from the market, or a tenth of the input price.
- `internal/api`:
  - the order hand, annotation, name;
  - the notes for an annotation the snapshot lacks and for deployments that disagree;
  - prices in credits ($2.80 is 280,000);
  - a newer snapshot with another price leaves the model's price as it was and shows `moved`;
  - prices typed by hand are `custom` and record no market.
- `internal/store`, on Postgres: migration 019, the annotation read back per model, `SetMarketID` with an entry, none and automatic, and a price's `market` kept through `SetPendingPrice` and `ListPrices`.
- `internal/kube`: the annotation is read from an LLMInferenceService, spaces trimmed.
- `hack/fetch-market.sh` on the live catalog, and `hack/check-market.sh` passing at 0 days and failing past its limit.

## Not tested

- The workflow `market` has not run on GitHub yet; it runs on the first Monday after the merge, or by hand from the Actions tab.
- No model on the lab or the sandbox hub is served under a Hugging Face id. The automatic match was only tested with test data; the annotation and the dialog are the ways to match those models.
