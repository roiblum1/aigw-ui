# 0.21.0 (not released yet)

Wallets are removed: a tenant's budgets are per model only, as before
0.20.0. One budget across every priced model did not match how budgets are
handed out, so the owner asked for per-model budgets back. What 0.20.0
added for wallets is taken out; everything after it (0.20.1 to 0.20.3)
stays.

Database: migration 024 drops `wallet_closures`, `quotas.wallet` and the
tenants' `wallet_credits` and `wallet_window`.

## Upgrading

- **A wallet bucket becomes the tenant's budget on its model**, of the same
  amount and window. The gateways already enforced it, so no limit on a
  gateway changes. A tenant that had a wallet keeps one budget per priced
  model, each the size of its old wallet: check them on its page and lower
  or remove the ones you do not want.
- **A spent wallet is open again** at once: its rule is gone from the
  SecurityPolicy at the next sync.
- **Rolling back** to 0.20.x after the upgrade needs the database from
  before it: 0.20.x reads the wallet columns that 024 drops.

## What changed

- **Gone:** the wallet on a tenant's page, the wallet on the tenants' own
  page and in the Tenants list, the hub service that added up wallet spend
  and closed tenants, the authorization rule a closure rendered, and the
  self-test step "A spent wallet refuses the tenant".
- **API:** `PUT` and `GET /tenants/{id}/wallet` are gone (404); a tenant no
  longer has `wallet` or `wallet_closed_until`, a quota no longer has
  `wallet`, `/my-usage` no longer has `wallet`, and the planner's `fleet` no
  longer has `wallets_day`.
- **The budget planner** adds up per-model budgets only. Split proposes
  equal budgets on one model, as it did; the "wallets come later" notes are
  gone.
- **Docs:** the user guide, architecture, capacity and API guides describe
  per-model budgets only. The design keeps its wallet sections as the record
  of what was built, marked removed.

## Tested

- **Units and store, on Postgres:** every Go test with the race detector,
  the migrations from empty, the API-server tests and the UI tests; the
  planner's figures worked out again without wallets.
- **Migration 024 on wallet data:** a database at 023 with a wallet tenant,
  its wallet bucket, another tenant's budget and a closure; after 024 the
  bucket is an ordinary budget of the same amount and window, the other
  budget is unchanged, and no wallet table or column is left.
- **An upgrade on a local hub:** 0.20.3 with the planner demo data gave a
  tenant a $1-a-day wallet (a bucket on demo-glm) and a closure; 0.21.0 on
  the same database showed demo-glm at $1.00 a day as the tenant's own
  budget, 404 for the wallet API, and the planner counting it. The Tenants,
  tenant, Capacity, Overview and tenant's own pages show no wallet, with no
  console errors and no overflow at 390 px.

## Not tested
