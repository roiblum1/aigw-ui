# 0.17.0 (2026-10-15)

The budget planner: how many dollars a day the fleet can still give, and
which model runs out first. Step 4 of the
[design](../design/capacity-prices-wallets.md). It builds on 0.16.0's
measurements of each LLMInferenceService.

No database change.

## What changed

- **The fleet line**, at the top of the Capacity page and on the Overview:
  what the fleet serves a day at the models' prices, what can be given, up
  to what with overcommit, what is given (wallets apart, once there are
  any), and what is left. A site a measurement drains still counts, so the
  line does not dip in the campaign window.
- **Which model runs out first.** Each priced model's busiest hour of the
  last 30 days over what it serves in an hour; above 0.85 it is marked
  saturated, the threshold the campaign uses to decide whether the other
  sites can take a drained site's load. The models in that order say where the next GPU helps most.
- **An overcommit suggestion over the fleet**, from the busiest day of the
  last 30, once there are two weeks of spend: between 1 and 5, applied by
  hand per model.
- **Split what is left** proposes equal daily budgets on one model for the
  tenants you pick, up to what is left; nothing changes until you apply it.
- **Yesterday's spend** on the Overview, at the models' prices, and with
  GPU costs what the GPUs cost a day.
- `internal/budget` holds the formulas, pure; `hack/seed-planner-demo.py`
  fills a hub with demo data for them.
- API: `GET /api/v1/budgets` gains `fleet`, `by_peak`, `market`, and per
  model `value_per_second`, `peak_hour_share`, `saturated` and `llmis`;
  `POST /api/v1/budgets/split`.

## Tested

- **Units, with figures worked out by hand** (`internal/budget`):
  - GLM at $2.80/$0.56/$8.80: an instance of 1,000 uncached, 9,000 cached and 100 output tokens a second is 872 credits a second; with 2 + 1 instances, 2,180; 113,011,200 can be given a day at 60%, 169,516,800 at 1.5×; a busiest hour of $70 is 0.892, saturated.
  - Qwen: 616 credits a second, 0.451 at its busiest hour.
  - The fleet: 241,574,400 a day; given 76,000,000 with a $10-an-hour wallet counted at 24 and its cap not added.
  - The suggestion: 2×, capped at 5×, held at 1×, none under two weeks.
  - The order by peak; Split rounding down.
- **Store, on Postgres:** the busiest hour per model with best-effort included, the busiest day over models, the days counted, a model in tokens left out, yesterday's spend.
- **A local hub with `hack/seed-planner-demo.py`:**
  - the API gave the same figures: demo-glm 2,180 a second, 0.892, saturated; demo-qwen 616, 0.451; the fleet $2,415.74 a day, $550 given, $1,624.17 left, a suggested 5× (the busiest day was $103);
  - the Capacity page and the Overview showed the line and the saturated mark;
  - Split proposed $50 a day each for two tenants from $100, and was cancelled;
  - no console errors, no overflow at 390 px.

## Not tested

- Wallets: none exist yet, so wallet spend and equal wallets in Split are not in it (step 5).
