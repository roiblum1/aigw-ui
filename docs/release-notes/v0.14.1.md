# 0.14.1 (2026-10-14)

Site weights from the measurements. The entry route shares a model's
traffic between its sites by their capacity, which until now came only from
the hand-set annotation `aigw-ui.io/capacity-per-instance`. The capacity
measurement gives the same figure, measured.

Database: migration 018 adds `model_endpoints.capacity_basis`.

## How it works

- **All measured, or not at all.** When every deployment of a model with a
  ready instance, on every site, has a measured shape, a site's capacity is
  its ready instances times what one instance of its shape was measured to
  serve, in thousands of input-equivalent tokens a second: the figure the
  Capacity page prices with. Otherwise the model stays on the annotation on
  every site, so its sites' numbers are always in one unit.
- **A new shape** that is not measured yet, such as a new
  `aigw-ui.io/model-revision`, puts the model back on the annotation until
  it is measured.
- **A switch is applied at once.** Going from the annotation to the
  measurements, or back, changes the unit, so the new capacity is taken as a
  first reading instead of rising one instance per poll. Within one basis
  the usual steps apply: up one instance per poll, down after two polls
  agree, drains as before.
- **The annotation** keeps working as the fallback, and for a model that is
  never measured nothing changes.

## Where it shows

- **Models page.** A site's weight says "measured"; its tooltip says where
  the capacity comes from, and the detail lists each deployment's ready
  instances and measured capacity.
- **API.** An endpoint's `capacity.basis` is `measured` or `declared`.
