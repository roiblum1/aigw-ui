# 0.12.1 (not released yet)

One fix, for models that a cluster's own chart exposes.

## A new quota on a discovered model was not counted

- A quota given to a tenant on a discovered model was written to the
  cluster and shown in the UI, but the gateway neither counted nor enforced
  it until the model's route happened to change. The tenant was served from
  the model's default budget in the meantime.
- Cause: a tenant's rule reaches the proxy only when its route is built
  again, and a changed `QuotaPolicy` does not make that happen. Routes the
  hub renders have carried an annotation for this since 0.9.2. Routes from a
  cluster's chart had none.
- The sync now sets `gateway.envoyproxy.io/aigw-ui-quota-revision` on the
  cluster's own `AIGatewayRoute` objects that send to a backend with a quota,
  before it writes the policy. It is the only field the hub writes on such a
  route. Helm and Argo CD keep an annotation the chart does not set.
- The first quota change on a model after the upgrade marks its route. A
  tenant whose quota was set before and is still not counted starts being
  counted then.

## Tested

On OpenShift 4.22 with Envoy Gateway 1.9.2 and AI Gateway 1.2.0, against
vLLM 0.29 on CPU with a 0.5B model, deployed as an `LLMInferenceService`
and exposed by a chart of its own:

- a tenant given a quota in the hub was counted on its next request;
  without the fix its counter stayed at 0;
- the self-test passed, 11 of 14 steps, the other three being for models
  with an entry route;
- a repeated prompt was charged for 512 cached and 43 new prompt tokens,
  exactly what vLLM reported, on `/v1/chat/completions`, streamed, and on
  `/anthropic/v1/messages`;
- a request with a forged `kv_transfer_params` was charged in full.
