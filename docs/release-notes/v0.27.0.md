# 0.27.0 (not released yet)

**All models** is one more choice in a tenant's quotas instead of a card of
its own, and a tenant has it or quotas per model, strictly: with a budget
for all models no quota can be added, on a model without prices either.

No database change.

## Upgrading

- Nothing to do. A tenant that has a budget for all models and quotas per
  model keeps both stored; its page marks the quotas **from before:
  remove**, with Remove only, and its budget cannot be changed until they
  are gone. On the sandbox hub, bolbol had $6 an hour for all models and 77
  tokens a day on `haiku`, a model without prices, which 0.25.0 and 0.26.0
  allowed.

## Changed

- **One card.** The tenant page's **Quotas** card holds the budget for all
  models too: **All models** is the first choice under **Add a quota for…**
  (in dollars, no dry run), and the budget is the first row of the table,
  with Edit, Reset usage and Remove. The **Budget across models** card is
  gone; **Models**, which models the tenant may call, stays a card.
- **Strictly one or the other.** **All models** cannot be picked while the
  tenant has any quota (the list says "remove the quotas below first"), and
  with all models the add form is closed. The hub refuses a quota on any
  model while the tenant has a budget for all models (HTTP 409), and the
  budget while the tenant has any quota, naming them. Before, quotas on
  models without prices were allowed beside it, since the budget does not
  count those models; with all models the tenant now draws from their
  pool.
