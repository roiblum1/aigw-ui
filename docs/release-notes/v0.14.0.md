# 0.14.0 (not released yet)

What is left to give. Budgets were set without seeing what the GPUs serve;
now every place where a budget is decided shows, for the model, what its
ready GPUs serve a day, the ceiling its overcommit limit allows, what the
tenants' budgets take and what is left. See
[capacity](../capacity.md#budgets-in-dollars).

Database: migration 017 adds `models.overcommit_limit` (1.5 by default).

## What is left to give

```
can be given   = what the measured, ready instances serve a day x expected busy share
ceiling        = can be given x overcommit limit
given          = the enabled tenants' enforced budgets, each at the most it allows in a day
left to give   = ceiling - given
```

- **Overcommit limit**, per model, 1.5 until you change it on the Capacity
  page next to the busy share. 1 is no overcommit.
- **Hourly and per-minute budgets** now count at the most they allow in a
  day: $1 an hour is up to $24. They were left out before, as rate limits,
  so a model's "given" can go up after the upgrade.
- **The shared pool** is shown beside the budgets, not added to them.
- The figures follow the fleet: a site that loses pods or is drained lowers
  them at the next discovery poll.
- **For a priced model, can be given is what its GPUs cost a day**: at the
  break-even prices, what the GPUs serve a day at the busy share is worth
  exactly their cost.

## Where it shows

- **A tenant's quotas.** Choosing a model in **Add a quota**, or editing a
  budget, shows the model's figures and the same with the amount being
  typed. Saving past the ceiling asks to confirm; nothing is refused. The
  card says what the tenant can use a day over the models in dollars.
- **Capacity page.** Each model's first panel: can be given, ceiling, what
  the budgets take, what is left, the pool, and the 30-day suggestion. Above
  the models, the total over the measured models in dollars.
- **Overview.** A **Budgets** card: one row per model and a total in
  dollars; a model not measured says what it was given against an unknown
  capacity.
- **Task log.** A budget change adds where the model stands, for example
  "glm budgets now $1,840 of $1,700 a day (1.08×, limit 1.5×)".

## The measured prices in one click

**Use these prices**, next to the break-even prices on the Capacity page,
opens the model's prices with them filled in and a note saying where they
come from. The usual rules apply: they start at the next 00:00 UTC, the first
prices convert the model's limits once, and the model starts in dry-run. The
Capacity page also gives the measured instances' cost a day.

## API

- `GET /api/v1/budgets`: per model `measured`, `capacity_day`,
  `ceiling_day`, `given_day`, `left_day`, `overcommit`, `overcommit_limit`,
  `short_windows`, `pool_day`; `credits` adds them up over the measured
  models in dollars.
- `PUT /api/v1/models/{id}/overcommit-limit` with `{"limit"}`, 1 to 10.
- `GET /api/v1/capacity`: the plan's `allocated_day` and `rate_limits` are
  replaced by `given_day`, `short_windows`, `overcommit_limit`,
  `ceiling_day`, `left_day` and `pool_day`.
