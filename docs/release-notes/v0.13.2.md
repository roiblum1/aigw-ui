# 0.13.2 (2026-10-14)

A cluster where the hub may not list pods or nodes says so, instead of
showing its GPUs as unlabelled.

No database change.

## Shapes on a cluster with a narrow ServiceAccount

Discovery reads the workload pods and the nodes' GPU labels to find each
deployment's GPU. A kubeconfig that may not list them is allowed, as
[security](../security.md) describes: the instances are still counted, and
the GPU is not known. Until now nothing said why, and the Capacity page then
asked for node labels that were already right.

- **Discovery** keeps a refusal to list pods or nodes. The cluster's
  discovery message on the Clusters page adds, for example, "not allowed to
  list pods or nodes, so the GPUs of its deployments are not known".
- **The Capacity page** names the permission next to the label and the GPU
  request as what the hub needs to read a GPU.

Found by the first measurement started from the sandbox hub: its account
on the lab, `aigw-ui-hub`, had been made before the capacity feature and
could list neither.

## Verified on the sandbox hub

The sandbox hub on 0.13.1, with `capacity.image` set to an AIPerf image in
the lab's registry, measured the lab's qwen-tiny end to end:

- Its account on the lab was given exactly what [security](../security.md)
  lists for measurements, and nothing more: list on pods and nodes across
  the cluster; create, get and delete on Jobs, list on Pods and get on
  `pods/log` in `vllm-lab`. The code makes no other call. It still may not
  read Secrets or create Jobs in other namespaces.
- **Measure** listed the one ready pod; the hub created
  `aigw-capacity-<id>`, which ran under OpenShift's `restricted-v2`, read
  its log every 20 seconds, and recorded three steps: 1, 2 and 4 in flight,
  all within the relaxed CPU targets, 26 to 73 prompt tokens a second,
  83% to 85% from the cache. The measurement succeeded after 11 minutes.
- The page showed 6.5M tokens a day for the instance, 3.9M at 60% busy,
  the chart and the steps, and its notes.
