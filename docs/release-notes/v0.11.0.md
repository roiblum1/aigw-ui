# 0.11.0 (not released yet)

The hub keeps what was used in the past, a tenant can see its own usage,
and best-effort use can be limited.

## Usage over time

- **A history.** Once a minute the hub reads the gateways' counters and
  writes what was used since its last look to Postgres, per tenant, model
  and hour. Until now it knew the running period only.
- **On the Overview**, under *Usage over time*: bars per day or per hour for
  the last 2, 7, 30 or 90 days, by tenant or by model, with the total, the
  average, the highest day and the part that was best-effort.
- **In dollars** for a model with prices, in tokens for the others. The two
  are never added up.
- The history starts with this version. Nothing from before is filled in.

## A page for tenants

- A tenant opens `/my-usage` and signs in with one of its API keys. It sees
  its budgets with what is used and left, its usage per hour and day, and
  the prices of its models.
- It sees its own tenant and nothing else, and can change nothing. The key
  opens this one page; the admin API refuses it.
- `config.tenantPage: false` turns it off. It is on by default.

## A limit on best-effort use

- A model that serves a spent budget as best-effort can limit how much a
  tenant uses that way in one period of its quota: **Best-effort limit** in
  the model's row. At the limit the tenant is refused until the period
  ends.
- Not run on a gateway yet. The loop and the database part are tested.

## No budget of three hours

A budget's period stays a minute, an hour or a day, which the gateway keeps
by itself, also while the hub is down. A block of three hours would have
to be kept by the hub and is not built.
[A budget per hour or per day](../pricing.md#a-budget-per-hour-or-per-day)
has the reasons.

## Upgrading

- The upgrade adds two tables and two columns. Nothing existing changes.
- `PUT /models/{id}/spent` now also sets `best_effort_limit`. A caller that
  leaves it out removes a limit that was set.
- New settings: `config.usageHistoryInterval` (`1m`; `0` keeps no history)
  and `config.tenantPage` (`true`).

## API

- New: `GET /usage/history`, `GET /my/usage`.
- `GET /models` has `best_effort_limit`. `GET /usage` has
  `best_effort_limit` and `best_effort_capped` on a quota. `GET /overage`
  has `capped_at`.

## Tested

On OpenShift 4.22 with Envoy Gateway 1.9.1 and AI Gateway 1.1.0, one site,
with a stand-in for the model server:

- A budget of $0.05 per hour: two requests answered, the third refused.
- A budget of $0.025 per minute: refused within the minute, answered in the
  next, with the hub doing nothing.
- The history equalled the counters over the end of two periods, after the
  hub was stopped while requests went on, and after a usage reset.
- A tenant read its own usage with its key. A wrong key and a tenant's key
  on the admin API got 401.
