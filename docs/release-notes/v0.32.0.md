# 0.32.0 (not released yet)

**Do not deploy 0.32.0: use 0.32.1.** Its `revoked-span` rule is CEL that
Envoy does not build, and a cluster's proxy then refuses every change the
hub makes (below, "After the merge").

Fixes for what the 0.31.0 end-to-end run found. The rule that counts budget
tenants on a model keeps its position, so the usage history no longer loses
their last use when an amount is added. The token policy refuses runs of
denied tokens by a span of their expiry instead of listing every token for
180 days. Quotas saved at the same time no longer fail with 409. The hub
syncs at start, and says how long it took to answer.

Database: migration 035 adds `models.budget_pass_slot`, the position of the
rule that lets a tenant with a budget across models through on a model. Each
model gets the position its rule has now, after its amounts; a new model
gets 0. A new amount takes the lowest free position other than that one.

## Upgrading

- Nothing moves on the clusters. On a local hub upgraded from 0.31.0 with a
  priced model, five amounts and the budget rule at position 5, the
  `QuotaPolicy` came out the same after the upgrade, its generation and the
  routes' quota revision unchanged.
- The hub syncs every cluster when it starts, and so once more after the
  upgrade.
- At that sync the token policy `aigw-ui-api-key-auth` changes shape: runs
  of three or more denied tokens become `revoked-span` rules. Envoy's own
  evaluation of that rule is not verified yet (below): after the upgrade,
  check on one cluster that a token of a tenant deleted with others is
  still refused (401 or 403).
- `GET /signing-keys` has a new field, `denied_spans`; `denied` still counts
  every denied token.

## Changed

- **The budget rule keeps its position.** On a model whose prices charge the
  budgets, the `QuotaPolicy` has a rule that lets a tenant with a budget
  across models through and counts what it used of the model, a counter per
  tenant named after the rule's position. It was the last rule, so it moved
  whenever an amount was added after it or the last one was freed. Use
  counted under the old position since the history's last look never
  reached the history, and the Usage page showed the tenant's use of the
  model start again from 0. It now has a position of its own, stored, like
  an amount's, and a new amount goes after it.
- **Denied tokens in spans of expiry.** A revoked token, and every token of a
  deleted tenant, was a `jti` in the token policy until it expired, 180 days
  by default: a test run that deletes its tenants left a line per token.
  Where three or more denied tokens follow one another by expiry and no
  live stored token expires among them, one entry `[from, to]` of a CEL
  rule refuses them all. 1,000 denials from 15 such deletions: 2.9 KB and
  80 lines of policy, against 33.6 KB and 1,113. A token signed in the last
  10 minutes, a token revoked on its own and a personal key's tokens are
  refused as before. Why a span refuses nothing else:
  [security](../security.md#tokens).
- **The hub syncs every cluster when it starts.** The first sync after a
  restart came with the 5-minute resync.
- **Quotas on one model are saved in turn.** A quota's position, and a new
  amount's, is the lowest free one, so two saved at the same moment picked
  the same; the store retried on the conflict, but eight writers at a time
  ran out of retries.

## Added

- **`Server-Timing: app;dur=<ms>`** on every admin API answer: the hub's own
  time until it started the answer, apart from the network's. An admin
  request that takes more than 2 seconds in all is logged as `slow request`.
- **The Tenants page** says in how many spans the token policy refuses the
  denied tokens.

## Fixed

- **The usage history lost a budget tenant's use of a model when the budget
  rule moved** (0.31.0 run, check 21: a tenant's day row held 5 of its 10
  requests; Redis had 5 under rule 4 and 5 under rule 9). The history reads
  a counter that went until its window ends, but only one it has looked at:
  the 5 requests came seconds after the tenant was made, and seeding moved
  the rule before the history's next look. The gateway's enforcement was not
  affected. What was lost before the upgrade is not added.
- **Saving quotas for many tenants at once failed now and then with 409
  `an item with that name already exists`** (since 0.24.0). Seeding 1,000
  tenants 8 at a time locally lost 1 quota of 500; 16 quotas with 8 new
  amounts on one model failed 3 runs of 3.
- The performance page said a sync writes in four stages; it is five since
  0.31.0.

## Tested

On 2026-10-17:

- `go test -race ./...` with Postgres 17 and Redis 7 (310 tests). New: the
  budget rule keeps its position and its counters' names when an amount is
  added after it and when amounts before it are freed
  (`TestBudgetPassRuleKeepsItsPosition`); a new amount skips the rule's
  stored position, on a new model and on one from before the migration
  (`TestQuotaAmounts`); 16 quotas with 8 new amounts saved at once
  (`TestQuotaAmountsAtOnce`, which failed 3 of 3 on 0.31.0 and passed 5 of 5);
  denials grouped into spans around live tokens, the most recent left out
  (`TestSpansOf`, `TestDeniedTokensInSpans` against Postgres); the span
  rule's CEL evaluated with cel-go, type checked and parsed only, on claims
  as doubles: a stored token inside a span refused, one a second outside
  it, a personal key's token and a request without a token let through
  (`TestIdentityDeniedSpanMatches`); spans split into rules of at most
  4,096 characters; `Server-Timing` on admin answers, gzipped or not, and
  not on a refused one.
- `hack/test-apiserver.sh` (Envoy Gateway v1.9.2 and Envoy AI Gateway
  v1.2.0 CRDs) and its v1.9.1 / v1.1.0 variant: both CRDs take two
  `revoked-span` rules of near 4,096 characters beside 130 `jti`s.
- A local hub (0.31.0, then this branch) against a local API server with the
  same CRDs, a priced model and the end-to-end run's 1,000 tenants (500 with
  own quotas of five amounts, 350 with budgets, 50 held to the model, 22
  tokens), seeded 8 at a time:

  | | 0.31.0 | 0.32.0 |
  |---|---|---|
  | Creating the 1,000 tenants (1,921 calls) | 2.0 s | 2.0 s |
  | `GET /tenants`, 288 KB / 35 KB gzipped | 4.6 ms | 5.7 ms |
  | `GET /usage`, 270 KB / 23 KB gzipped | 12 ms | 14 ms |

  The end-to-end run's 132.6 s and 1.3 to 2.9 s were the link from the test
  machine (0.53 s for `/healthz`, no gzip), not the hub; neither version
  changes them ([performance](../performance.md#the-admin-api)).
- The upgrade on that hub: migration 035 set the model's position to 5; the
  first sync after the start, 2 s after it, left the `QuotaPolicy`, its
  generation and the route's revision as they were. A new amount then took
  position 6 and the budget rule stayed at 5.
- The 22 tokens of the deleted tenants: 22 `jti`s in the policy on 0.31.0,
  one span after the upgrade's first sync.
- `go vet`, staticcheck v0.8.2, `hack/check-duplication.sh`,
  `hack/check-chart.sh`, `hack/check-release-note.sh`; the web tests,
  `tsc` and the build.

Not run:

- Anything on a gateway. Above all, Envoy's evaluation of the `revoked-span`
  rule: a span that does not match lets its tokens through. To check: after
  the upgrade, a token of a tenant deleted with two others is refused, and
  a live token that expires next to them is answered.
- The history fix against a gateway's counters (check 21 of the end-to-end
  run), and the read APIs timed from the test machine with gzip and
  `Server-Timing`.

After the merge, on 2026-10-17, the sandbox hub ran 0.32.0 (image
`sha-0c0ccf76e7cb`, Helm revision 62) against the lab. The self-test failed:
9 passed, 5 failed, 1 warning, 5 skipped. A revoked token and a revoked
personal key's token were still answered 90 s after the sync; a new
quota's counters never appeared and requests over it were answered; a
request was not counted for its token's tenant. The lab's policy showed the
`revoked-span` rule and `Accepted`. The cause: Envoy builds CEL without
comprehensions and without joining strings, and the rule used `exists()`
and `sub + '.' + jti`; Envoy 1.39.1 refuses such a configuration whole
(`failed to create an expression: Comprehension support is disabled`,
reproduced locally), so the lab's proxy took none of the hub's changes after
the upgrade's first sync. cel-go, the API server's CRD and every test took
the rule. Fixed in 0.32.1.
