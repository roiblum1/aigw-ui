# 0.32.1 (not released yet)

Fixes 0.32.0's token policy, which stopped a cluster's proxy from taking any
change. 0.32.0 refused runs of denied tokens with a CEL rule that used a
comprehension (`exists()`) and joined strings; Envoy builds CEL without
either and refused the whole route configuration, so after the upgrade no
revoke, new quota or other change reached the lab's proxy, while Envoy
Gateway showed the policy `Accepted`. The rule is now a chain of
comparisons that Envoy 1.39.1 builds and enforces, tested on a real Envoy.

Database: no migration.

## Upgrading

- From 0.32.0: the hub's first sync replaces the `revoked-span` rules, and
  the proxy then takes every change it refused since 0.32.0's sync: revokes,
  quotas, models. Check the self-test passes, and that the proxy's
  `update_rejected` counters stop growing ([operations](../operations.md#troubleshooting)).
- From 0.31.0 or earlier: as 0.32.0's upgrade, without its fault.

## Fixed

- **The `revoked-span` rule is CEL Envoy builds.** Each span is
  `exp >= from && exp <= to` on the token's claims, joined with `||`; a
  stored token is told from a personal key's by `cid.endsWith(jti)` instead
  of `cid == sub + '.' + jti`. Envoy turns comprehensions off and does not
  join strings unless told to (Envoy 1.39 `filters/common/expr/evaluator.cc`),
  and Envoy Gateway does not tell it.
- **A span stands for ten or more denied tokens**, not three: an entry of two
  comparisons is about as long as eight `jti`s. 1,000 denials from 15
  deletions of test tenants make 5.3 KB and 99 lines of policy (0.32.0's
  form: 2.9 KB; as `jti`s: 33.6 KB and 1,113 lines). A rule holds about 19
  spans.

## Added

- **The tests check every authorization rule in CEL for what Envoy builds**
  (`TestAuthorizationCELForEnvoy`): no comprehension, and only the
  operators and string tests Envoy has. 0.32.0's rule fails it.

## Tested

On 2026-10-17:

- Envoy v1.39.1 (`docker.io/envoyproxy/envoy:v1.39.1`, the lab's proxy
  version) in a container, with the hub's JWT provider (inline key set,
  claims in metadata under `aigw-hub`) and the span rules in the form Envoy
  Gateway 1.9.2 gives them (an RBAC matcher with a CEL predicate on the
  parsed expression), and tokens signed by the hub's token package:
  - 0.32.0's rule: Envoy did not start, `failed to create an expression:
    Comprehension support is disabled`.
  - 0.32.1's rule, two spans: 403 for stored tokens inside a span, at its
    first and its last second, and in a span of one second; 200 for stored
    tokens a second before and after, one between the spans, and a personal
    key's token inside a span; 401 without a token.
  - 42 spans in three rules of 3,998, 3,998 and 1,694 characters: the same,
    with 403 in the last span and 200 between two of them.
- `go test -race ./...` with Postgres 17 and Redis 7; `hack/test-apiserver.sh`
  and its v1.9.1 / v1.1.0 variant (two `revoked-span` rules of near 4,096
  characters taken); the web and client tests; `go vet`, staticcheck v0.8.2,
  `hack/check-duplication.sh`, `hack/check-chart.sh`,
  `hack/check-release-note.sh`.

Not run:

- The lab after the upgrade: the self-test, a revoke, and check 19b (a token
  of a deleted test tenant refused, the proxy's `update_rejected` counters
  still).
