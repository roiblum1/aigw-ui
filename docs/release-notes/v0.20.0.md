# 0.20.0 (not released yet)

Wallets: one budget for a tenant across every model with prices, per hour
or per day, enforced by the hub. Step 5c of the
[design](../design/capacity-prices-wallets.md#identity-and-quota-rules),
the fallback it describes, since the gateway's own enforcement (lab check
A5) could not be checked yet.

Database: migration 023 adds a tenant's wallet, marks the quotas the hub
keeps equal to a wallet, and records the tenants whose wallet is spent.

## Upgrading

Nothing changes until a tenant is given a wallet. Wallets need Redis and
the usage history (`config.usageHistoryInterval`, on by default); without
them a wallet cannot be set.

## What changed

- **A wallet** on a tenant's page: an amount in dollars per hour (from the
  hour) or per day (from 00:00 UTC), across every priced model. The tenant's
  quotas on priced models become caps inside it; on each priced model it
  has no cap on, it gets a bucket the hub keeps equal to the wallet. Models
  without prices are not in it.
- **A spent wallet** closes the tenant until its window ends: the gateways
  refuse it on every priced model (HTTP 403), or serve it as best-effort
  where a model does that for a spent budget. The rule names the window's
  end, so it ends by itself, also while the hub is down.
- **How it is enforced:** every `OVERAGE_INTERVAL` (15 s) the hub adds up
  each wallet tenant's spend in its window from the usage history, which
  records once a minute, and closes it at its wallet. A tenant can go past
  its wallet by about a minute of use plus a sync. While the hub is down a
  spent wallet is not noticed, and each priced model still stops the tenant
  at its wallet bucket: in the worst case, the wallet once per priced model
  in that window.
- **The tenants' own page** shows the wallet, what was spent and by model,
  and whether it is spent; the Tenants list marks wallets and spent ones.
- **The budget planner** counts a wallet once, at its daily maximum, with
  its buckets and caps inside it.
- **Self-test:** "A spent wallet refuses the tenant" gives the temporary
  tenant a wallet of 1 credit, which its first request spent, and waits for
  HTTP 403.
- **API:** `PUT` and `GET /tenants/{id}/wallet`; a tenant has `wallet` and
  `wallet_closed_until`; a quota has `wallet`.

## Tested

- **Units:** the rule a closure renders (the window's end, the client ID's
  prefix, the models, escaping a quote), first of the rules with API keys
  and with tokens, split over several rules for many models; the service
  closes a tenant at its wallet, starts best-effort on the models that serve
  a spent budget so, leaves one below its wallet or closed already, syncs
  once and not at all when nothing changed; the planner counts a wallet
  once.
- **Store, on Postgres:** buckets on priced models only, following the
  wallet, a hand-set quota made a cap and kept one, the bucket back when
  the cap goes, all gone with the wallet; spend from the history in the
  window and in credits only; a closure on the models not in best-effort,
  ended with its window.
- **A local hub with Redis and the history on:** a tenant given a $1-a-day
  wallet got its bucket; $1.50 of recorded spend closed it within one tick,
  the SecurityPolicy it would apply held the rule, the wallet API and the
  tenant's own page showed it spent until 00:00 UTC; screenshots at 1440
  and 390 px, no console errors.

## Not tested

- **On a real gateway:** that the rule sees the model name and refuses with
  403 (the lab login expired), and the self-test step.
- **The best-effort path** of a spent wallet on a real gateway.
- **Real spend:** the spend was written into the history by hand; the
  gateway's counters to the history to the wallet were not run end to end.
