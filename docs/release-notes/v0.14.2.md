# 0.14.2 (2026-10-15)

Prices set by hand are the normal path, and GPU costs are optional. A model
has always had one price, the same on every site and whichever GPU serves the
request; the pages now say so, and no longer ask for GPU prices as if the
model's prices depended on them.

No database change.

## Prices

- **The Prices dialog** lists the other models' prices next to the ones
  being typed, dearest first, with each input price as a multiple of the
  cheapest. Setting GLM above Kimi above Qwen is then a matter of reading
  the order. Prices waiting for their day are shown in place of those in
  use.
- **The Models page** shows a priced model's input price, for example
  "$10.00 / 1M in", in place of the word "priced".
- **The pricing guide** starts with one price per model and setting prices by
  hand: the order and the ratios matter more than the amounts, and the level
  can follow what the fleet costs a day or be a unit of budget. The
  arithmetic from GPU costs is the optional check after it.

## GPU costs

- **Renamed** from GPU prices, so they are not taken for what tenants pay.
  The API stays `/gpu-prices`.
- **Optional.** The warning about GPUs without a price is gone. A model with
  no GPU costed gets no note; one with some costed and some not does, since
  its cost would leave some out. The panel says what GPU costs would add.
- **Prices and cost**, the model's panel on the Capacity page, says when the
  model has no prices and where to give them, and presents the break-even
  prices as a check for hand-set prices, or a start.

## Site weights

The detail of a site whose weight comes from the measurements gives three
figures: "1 × 0.0272", where it said "1 × 0.03".
