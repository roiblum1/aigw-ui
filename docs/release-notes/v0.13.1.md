# 0.13.1 (not released yet)

A capacity measurement checks that its prompts fit the model before it
starts.

No database change.

## The model's length

The form fits the longest prompt to the annotation
`aigw-ui.io/max-model-len` on the deployment. A deployment without it, such
as one that passes `--max-model-len` in `VLLM_ADDITIONAL_ARGS`, was offered
AIPerf's 167,000 tokens, and the model refused every request: the
measurement ended with "no load met the targets" and a step full of errors.
Found on the lab, where qwen-tiny takes 8,192 tokens and declares nothing.

- **The Job asks the pod.** Before it makes up any session, the Job reads
  the pod's `/v1/models`, where vLLM lists the longest request it takes.
  When the longest prompt and answer do not fit, it stops at once and the
  measurement fails with what to set, for example "the model takes at most
  8,192 tokens, prompt and answer together, and the settings ask for up to
  168,500: set the longest prompt to at most 6,692".
- **The form says so.** For a deployment without the annotation, the
  deployment field says that its length is not declared and that the Job
  checks it.
- A model server that does not list its length is measured as before.

## The Capacity page

Seen with the first measurement driven by the hub on the lab:

- **Width.** The table of shapes no longer scrolls sideways on a laptop
  screen: long column titles take two lines, and a measurement's steps
  scroll in their own box instead of widening the table.
- **A shape whose GPU is not known**, such as one on nodes without the
  label `nvidia.com/gpu.product`, no longer asks for a GPU price that
  cannot be set; the note says what the nodes and pods need.
- **While a measurement runs**, the banner says how many sessions were made
  up and how long their prompts grow before the first step ends, instead
  of "making up the sessions".
- A model with no measured instance shows "Nothing yet" alone, without a
  line of zeros under it.

## Verified on the lab

The hub, from the merged 0.13.0 code, with the lab registered: discovery
read the deployment's shape (one node, revision, one ready instance, no
GPU label on a CPU lab), **Measure** listed its one ready pod out of 66,
the Job ran under OpenShift's `restricted-v2`, and the hub followed its
three steps to a result of 4 in flight at a relaxed CPU target, with 75%
to 81% of prompt tokens from the cache. With this release, a measurement
with the default settings against the same 8,192-token model failed after
17 seconds with the message above.
